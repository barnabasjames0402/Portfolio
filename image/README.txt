Drop your files in here using these exact names and the site

falls back to a placeholder, so nothing breaks.


>>> portrait.png  —  READ THIS ONE PROPERLY <<<
-----------------------------------------------
This is the photo that stands in front of the giant
PORTFOLIO letters on the front page.

It MUST have a transparent background. If you use a normal
rectangular JPG, you'll see a visible box sitting on top of
the letters and the whole effect collapses.

How to cut yourself out:
  Photoshop   Select > Subject, then Select > Inverse, then
              Delete. Save As > PNG (not JPG).
  Free option Upload to remove.bg, download the PNG.

What makes a good one:
  - Stand facing slightly side-on, like the reference video.
  - Shoot against a plain wall. Cut-outs are far cleaner
    when the background isn't busy.
  - Crop from roughly the knees or waist up. The bottom of
    the image should be where you want it to meet the
    bottom of the screen.
  - Around 1200 x 1600px, saved as PNG.
  - Don't leave empty transparent space below your feet, or
    your photo will float above the bottom edge.

TIPS
----
- Keep each file under about 400KB or the site crawls on
  mobile data. Run them through squoosh.app first.
- Use .jpg for photos and mockups, .png for logos and cut-outs.
- Name files in lowercase with no spaces. "logo-1.png" works,
  "Logo 1.PNG" will break on a live server even though it
  works fine on your laptop.
- Drop your CV in the main folder as resume.pdf so the
  "Download CV" button works.
